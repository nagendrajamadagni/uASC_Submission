Analysis at basic-block budget MaxBB=10 (double the default). Each function's documented secret pointer is pre-seeded at entry; the tool reports every indirect branch reached while a secret or secret-derived register is live. CAST_set_key exceeded the 300 s per-entry cap at O2/O3 (path explosion) and is marked timed out — that is “not analyzed,” not “no gadget.” PLT stubs are counted as indirect branches. At MaxBB=5 the same GCC sweep found 57 instances across 15 functions; deepening to 10 yields 264 across 34.
